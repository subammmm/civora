⚠️ Important Notes

  • All investment amounts and timelines are subject to change. Verify current requirements on official government websites.
  • Consider tax implications in your home country before proceeding with any citizenship program.
  • Some countries may not allow dual citizenship - check your current citizenship laws.
  • Professional legal and tax advice is recommended for any citizenship investment above $100,000.
