⚠️ Important Considerations

  • Due Diligence: All programs require background checks and clean criminal records
  • Tax Implications: Consider tax obligations in both home and destination countries
  • Professional Advice: Consult immigration lawyers and tax advisors before proceeding
  • Processing Times: Timelines can vary based on application volumes and individual circumstances
  • Investment Risks: Real estate and investment options carry market risks
