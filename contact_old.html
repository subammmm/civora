Our Vision

Civora aims to be the most trusted, comprehensive resource for Nepali students pursuing international education. We believe that access to accurate information should never be a barrier to achieving educational dreams.

Through research-driven content, verified scholarships, and practical tools, we're building pathways that connect talented students with global opportunities.
