About Civora

Civora was founded to bridge the information gap for Nepali and underprivileged students seeking global opportunities. By compiling verified scholarships, visa pathways, and citizenship options, Civora serves as a research hub to support students in building international futures.

Our Vision

Civora began as a research hub for Nepali students, but its mission reaches far beyond one country. We envision Civora evolving into a global mobility network — a platform where students and professionals worldwide can access transparent, affordable guidance on scholarships, visas, tax residency, and citizenship strategies.

  • Global Contributors: Researchers and advisors across regions, sharing verified updates.
  • Interactive Tools: Scholarship matching, visa timelines, and residency calculators.
  • Affordable Guidance: Free resources for all, with optional paid services for deeper support.
  • Scalable Impact: From Nepal to the world, Civora empowers underrepresented students and professionals to design their own futures.
