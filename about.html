About Civora

Civora was founded to bridge the information gap for Nepali and underprivileged students seeking global opportunities. By compiling verified scholarships, visa pathways, and citizenship options, Civora serves as a research hub to support students in building international futures.

Founder's Story

Hi, I'm Shubham Dhakal, a Nepali student passionate about making global education accessible to underrepresented communities. As someone who has navigated the complex world of international scholarships and visa applications, I understand firsthand the challenges that Nepali students face when seeking opportunities abroad.

My journey began with countless hours researching scholarships, deciphering visa requirements, and trying to understand the pathways to study and work internationally. Through this process, I realized that most information was scattered, outdated, or simply inaccessible to students from developing countries like Nepal.

Civora is my solution to this problem. What started as personal research notes has evolved into a comprehensive platform designed to democratize access to global opportunities. This project represents my commitment to:

  • Research Excellence: Verifying every scholarship and pathway through official sources
  • Web Development Skills: Building accessible, multilingual tools for global reach
  • Leadership Through Service: Creating solutions that benefit entire communities
  • Global Impact: Connecting local challenges with international opportunities

Through creating Civora, I've demonstrated my technical and leadership capabilities while making a meaningful contribution to global educational equity. This platform showcases my ability to identify real-world problems and develop comprehensive solutions that serve entire communities.

Our Vision

Civora began as a research hub for Nepali students, but its mission reaches far beyond one country. We envision Civora evolving into a global mobility network — a platform where students and professionals worldwide can access transparent, affordable guidance on scholarships, visas, backdoor tax residency in low-tax countries, and free citizenship pathway options.

  • Global Contributors: Researchers and advisors across regions, sharing verified updates.
  • Interactive Tools: Scholarship matching, visa timelines, and residency calculators.
  • Tax Optimization: Help individuals set up strategic tax residency in low-tax jurisdictions.
  • Free Citizenship Pathways: Guidance on accessible routes to citizenship through investment, ancestry, or naturalization.
  • Affordable Guidance: Free resources for all, with optional paid services for deeper support.
  • Scalable Impact: From Nepal to the world, Civora empowers underrepresented students and professionals to design their own futures.

Our Methodology

Civora's strength lies in our rigorous research methodology and commitment to accuracy. Every piece of information on our platform goes through a multi-step verification process:

01

Primary Source Research

We collect information directly from official government websites, embassy portals, university admissions offices, and scholarship providers. No secondary sources or unverified claims are accepted.

02

Direct Verification

Our team contacts institutions directly via email, phone, or official inquiry forms to confirm details about application processes, deadlines, and eligibility criteria.

03

Continuous Updates

We maintain regular communication with our network of contributors and institutional contacts to ensure information remains current and accurate throughout the year.

04

Community Feedback

Students who use our resources provide feedback about their experiences, helping us identify outdated information and improve our guidance continuously.
