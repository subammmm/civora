Financial Proof Checklist for Student Visa

Essential documents to demonstrate financial capacity

Important: Requirements vary by country and university. Always verify specific requirements with the embassy and institution.

📋 Required Documents

☐ Bank Statements (6-12 months)
Original stamped statements showing consistent balance and transaction history
☐ Bank Solvency Certificate
Official letter from bank confirming account balance and standing
☐ Sponsor Letter
Detailed letter confirming financial support (if applicable)
☐ Income Tax Returns
Last 2-3 years of tax returns for sponsor/student
☐ Employment Verification
Letter from employer confirming position and salary
☐ Fixed Deposit Certificates
Certificates showing long-term savings and investments
☐ Property Valuation Documents
Property ownership certificates and valuation reports
☐ Scholarship Award Letters
Official letters confirming any scholarships received
☐ Education Loan Approval
Bank approval letter for education loan (if applicable)
☐ Relationship Proof
Documents proving relationship between sponsor and student

💰 Financial Requirements by Destination

Australia: AUD 21,041/year for living costs + tuition
Canada: CAD 10,000-15,000/year + tuition
UK: £1,334/month (London), £1,023/month (outside London) + tuition
USA: USD 15,000-25,000/year + tuition
Germany: €10,332/year for blocked account + tuition

✅ Before Submission

☐ All documents are original or certified copies
☐ Bank statements are recent (within 1 month)
☐ Currency conversion calculations included
☐ All documents translated to English (if required)
☐ Financial capacity exceeds minimum requirements by 20%
Pro Tip: Maintain consistent high balances in accounts for 3-6 months before application to show financial stability.
